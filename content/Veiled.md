### Class Description
The **Veiled** is a risk-reward support hero whose necromantic powers grow stronger as his own life slips away. Cursed with an undying body and yearning for the release of death, he sacrifices his own vitality to unleash devastating attacks and miraculous healing at the brink of oblivion.

## Combat Skills

The Veiled's combat skills revolve around controlled self-sacrifice. As his health declines, his abilities gain increasingly powerful effects, allowing him to heal allies, stun enemies, and ultimately unlock devastating techniques while standing upon Death's Door.
